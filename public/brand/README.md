# St. Catharines Digital logo

The SC monogram is the primary brand mark. Its blue corner suggests a folded
newspaper page. Pair it with the two-line serif wordmark for mastheads and use
the monogram alone for avatars and small icons.

## Files

- `logo-ink.svg` / `logo-ink.png`: transparent logo for light backgrounds.
- `logo-paper.svg` / `logo-paper.png`: transparent logo for dark backgrounds.
- `monogram-ink.*` / `monogram-paper.*`: transparent square marks.
- `monogram-ink-mono.svg` / `monogram-paper-mono.svg`: single-colour marks.
- `icon-192.png` / `icon-512.png`: opaque paper tiles for app icons.

SVGs contain outlined lettering and need no installed font. Import an SVG into
Canva for scalable artwork, or use a PNG when an upload requires a raster image.
The PNG wordmarks are 2160 × 512; monograms are 1024 × 1024.

## Use

Ink: `#172128`. Paper: `#f7f4ed`. Blue: `#234bb5`.
Use the ink logo on paper or white and the paper logo on ink. Preserve the
aspect ratio and leave at least one blue-corner-width of space around the mark.
Avoid stretching, effects, or adding a background to the transparent artwork.
Use the square mark below a 200 px wordmark width. At 16 px, treat it as a
recognition symbol rather than readable lettering.

The wordmark and monogram use outlined Fraunces lettering. Its SIL Open Font
License is included in `OFL.txt`.
