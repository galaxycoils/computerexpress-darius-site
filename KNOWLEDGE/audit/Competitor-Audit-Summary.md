# Competitor SEO Audit — St. Catharines

## Competitors Analyzed
1. **Niagara Web Design**
   - **Strengths:** Strong domain age, clear local SEO focus, service-page granularity.
   - **Gaps:** Outdated design feel, slow mobile performance compared to our 100/100 score.
2. **WebWorx Niagara**
   - **Strengths:** High volume of testimonials, active local business community presence.
   - **Gaps:** Poor mobile usability, lack of modern Schema markup.
3. **St. Catharines Marketing Pros**
   - **Strengths:** Broad SEO services, good content density.
   - **Gaps:** Generic branding, slow page load times, no clear JTBD positioning.

## Key Takeaways
- None of our competitors are leveraging Schema markup at our level.
- None have our performance score (Lighthouse 100).
- **Opportunity:** Position ourselves as the "Technical/Performance Leader" for service businesses, focusing on the high-tension visuals identified in our GalaxyCoils strategy.
