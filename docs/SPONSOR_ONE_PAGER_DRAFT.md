SLIDE 5 — WHY SPONSOR
---------------------
Differentiation vs. Facebook / Reddit / secondary news:
- Official sources only — every notice links to original document
- No social media noise, no unverified rumors
- No maps, no lists of individuals, no speculation
- Trust as the moat — sources badge on every entry

What sponsors get:
- Header placement in weekly Planning Alert email (before "upcoming meetings")
- Dedicated sponsor card on Planning Tracker page (sidebar or below results)
- Branded callout in "Upcoming Meetings" section ("Presented by [Sponsor]")
- Optional: logomark next to each notice category in table view
- Monthly performance email: opens, clicks, top search terms, unsubscribe rate

What we don't do:
- No sponsored content that disguises opinion as news
- No listing of individuals, no maps of private addresses
- No editing of official notices
- No bundling sponsor messages into the NRPS crime coverage

SLIDE 7 — EDITORIAL INDEPENDENCE
---------------------------------
This matters. Here's the policy:

- Sponsors have no say in which notices we cover
- Sponsors are not listed next to specific applications (no "Sponsored by X on this variance")
- Sponsor placement is structural (header, sidebar card), not editorial
- Every notice page includes "Sourced from [municipality].ca" with direct link
- NRPS releases are covered under the same no-interference rule
- We publish a "Sponsors" page on the site listing current sponsors + policy
- If a sponsor is involved in an active planning application, we disclose the relationship and do not place their ad on that specific notice page (or we drop the search-term sponsorship for that application)
