# St. Catharines Digital newsroom redesign research

## Product direction

This release prioritizes everyday Niagara residents: fast scanning, clear civic context, practical local information and source links that remain close to each claim.

## Competitive observations

The public search result for St. Catharines Digital currently describes a website and SEO service, even though the page also contains local planning records. The redesigned first screen therefore makes the newsroom identity explicit. [St. Catharines Digital](https://stcatharinesdigital.ca/)

ThoroldToday leads with a broad local-news front page, frequent stories, events, weather and newsletter-oriented utility. St. Catharines Digital differentiates through structured civic coverage: council, planning, police releases, elections and primary documents. [ThoroldToday](https://www.thoroldtoday.ca/)

Established regional publications include the St. Catharines Standard, Niagara This Week and the Welland Tribune. The redesign does not attempt to imitate a full general newspaper; it makes source-first local context the core product. [St. Catharines Standard](https://www.stcatharinesstandard.ca/), [Niagara This Week](https://www.niagarathisweek.com/), [Welland Tribune](https://www.wellandtribune.ca/)

## Implemented decisions

- Newsroom-first hero and hierarchy.
- Resident-oriented calls to action: latest news and planning tracker.
- Navigation that exposes News, Council, Planning, Votes, Police and Guides.
- City shortcuts for St. Catharines, Welland and Thorold.
- Restrained teal civic signal colour, deep ink and paper-toned surfaces.
- Responsive hero and topic navigation for mobile scanning.
- Existing restored votes, policy, corrections, guide, alert and membership routes are preserved.
