## 2. Target Audience

The site covers municipal planning notices, council decisions, and police releases. The buyers are people who need to know what's happening in Niagara municipalities before others do.

**Primary targets:**

1. **Real estate brokerages** — agents need to know about zoning changes, development applications, and planning approvals that affect property values and client advice. St. Catharines, Welland, Thorold, Niagara Region all have active markets.

2. **Real estate developers** — they need advance notice of planning applications, public meetings, zoning changes, and council decisions that affect their projects or competition.

3. **Construction companies** — project awareness, bidding opportunities, municipal contracts, infrastructure notices.

4. **Law firms (municipal/planning law)** — clients come to them for planning applications, appeals, zoning issues. They need to track what's happening to advise clients.

5. **Architects and engineering firms** — track development applications, planning meetings, infrastructure projects they can work on.

6. **Home builders** — track subdivision approvals, zoning changes, new development applications.

7. **Commercial real estate firms** — office, retail, industrial properties affected by municipal decisions.

8. **Mortgage brokers** — less direct, but their developer/investor clients need this information.

**Secondary targets:**

9. **Chamber of commerce** — broader community awareness, member value
10. **Business improvement areas (BIAs)** — local business advocacy, development awareness

## 6. Outreach Cadence

**Day 1-3:** Send initial emails to all 30 prospects
**Day 4-5:** Follow up by phone or LinkedIn to anyone who hasn't replied
**Day 6-8:** Send second follow-up email with sample digest
**Day 9-10:** Phone follow-up to highest-priority prospects
**Day 11-14:** Final follow-up, offer to meet

**Tracking:**
- Every outreach attempt logged in a spreadsheet or CRM
- Track: date, prospect name, contact, email sent, response, next step
- Categories: no response, interested, not interested, proposal requested, paid

**Day 14 gate:**
- ≥10 substantive replies (not just "thanks" — actual interest or questions)
- ≥3 proposal requests
- ≥1 paid commitment at the stated pilot price

If we hit these, we scale. If not, we analyze what went wrong and adjust the pitch or target list.
